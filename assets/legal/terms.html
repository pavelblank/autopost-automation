Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

AutoPost is a personal, single-user automation tool. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept sign-ups from other users.

What it does

AutoPost generates short video, image, and text content and posts it to social media accounts that are owned and controlled by the tool's operator. When connected to TikTok, it uploads pre-made videos to the operator's own TikTok drafts using the Content Posting API. Nothing is published without the operator's separate action inside the TikTok app.

Who can use it

This tool is used solely by its own operator, on their own accounts. It is not distributed, resold, or made available to third parties.

Data

AutoPost does not collect, sell, or share data belonging to any person other than its own operator. See the Privacy Policy for details.
