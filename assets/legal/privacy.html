Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AutoPost is a personal automation tool operated by a single individual for their own social media accounts. It is not a public service.

What data is used

AutoPost uses OAuth access tokens (issued by YouTube, TikTok, and other connected platforms) solely to publish content to the operator's own accounts on those platforms. No data belonging to any other person is collected, stored, or processed.

Where data is stored

All access tokens and generated content are stored locally on the operator's own device. Nothing is transmitted to or stored by any third party beyond the connected platforms themselves (e.g. TikTok, YouTube).

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
